# satzivo A2 flashcard bank

## Verified inventory

The versioned shared catalogue contains **122 complete cards**. Personal words saved from the dictionary are returned separately for each user and are not counted toward the shared target.

| Type | Available | Target | Remaining |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A2 vocabulary words | 70 | 600 | 530 |
| Full communication expressions | 52 | 200 | 148 |
| **Total unique cards** | **122** | **800** | **678** |

The first authored batch adds 52 words and 50 expressions. It adds two original A2 examples to every card, translations in Spanish and English, skill/topic labels, a priority and difficulty tag, and noun or verb metadata where applicable. Cloze prompts are checked for each card. Headwords, examples, and cloze prompts are checked for duplicates.

## Data source

The Goethe-Institut adult A2 Wortliste is used as a level and topic reference. The app's translations, expressions, and example sentences are independently authored; the Wortliste's entries and examples are not copied into the catalogue.

Reference: [Goethe-Zertifikat A2 Wortliste](https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/en/Goethe-Zertifikat_A2_Wortliste.pdf).
